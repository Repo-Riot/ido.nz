About

Hi, I'm Alex - the planner, cheerleader, spreadsheet-slayer, and romantic heart behind I Do Wedding & Event Planning.

With over two decades in the world of events, I've learned that the true magic of a wedding isn't just in how it looks, it's in how it feels. My love of organisation, design, and detail has grown into something far more meaningful: helping couples create celebrations that feel effortless, personal, and completely their own.

As an independent planner and coordinator, I work closely with you to shape a day that reflects who you are, what you love, and how you want to celebrate. Every recommendation and decision is guided by your vision, your priorities, and the atmosphere you want to create.

My role is to be your calm in the chaos. Whether you're planning an intimate elopement, a grand celebration, or something beautifully in between, I'll guide you through the process, keep the details elegantly organised, and ensure the experience feels just as special as the day itself.

Because your wedding should be a true reflection of you - thoughtful, meaningful, and full of heart. Watching it all come together, knowing I helped bring it to life, is a privilege I never take for granted, and it's why I truly love what I do.
