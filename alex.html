Meet Alex

Founder & Creative Force Behind I Do Wedding & Event Planning

Hi, I'm Alex - the planner, cheerleader, spreadsheet-slayer, and romantic heart behind I Do Wedding & Event Planning.

With over two decades in the world of events, I've learned that the true magic of a wedding isn't just in how it looks - it's in how it feels. My love of organisation, design, and detail has evolved into something much bigger: helping couples create celebrations that feel effortless, personal, and completely their own.

As an independent planner, I work for you - not a venue - which means every recommendation and decision is made with your best day in mind. My focus is on making your celebration extraordinary within your budget, while ensuring you feel calm, confident, and cared for at every stage.

My role? To be your calm in the chaos. Whether it's an intimate elopement, a grand celebration, or something perfectly in between, I'll guide you through the process, keep the details in check, and make sure you actually enjoy the journey - not just survive it.

Because your wedding should be a reflection of you - beautiful, meaningful, and full of love. Seeing it all come together, knowing I helped bring it to life, is what fills my cup every single time.
