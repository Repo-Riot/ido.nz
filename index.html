Meet Alex

Hi, I’m Alex, the planner, cheerleader, spreadsheet-slayer, and romantic heart behind I Do Wedding & Event Planning.

With over two decades in the world of events, I’ve learned that the true magic of a wedding isn’t just in how it looks, it’s in how it feels. My love of organisation, design, and detail has grown into something far more meaningful: helping couples create celebrations that feel effortless, personal, and completely their own.

As an independent planner and coordinator, I work closely with you to shape a day that reflects who you are, what you love, and how you want to celebrate. My role is to be your calm in the chaos, whether you’re planning an intimate elopement, a grand celebration, or something beautifully in between.

Because your wedding should be a true reflection of you: thoughtful, meaningful, and full of heart.
