Services

Thoughtfully tailored wedding planning, delivered with calm confidence. My service options are designed to offer flexible levels of support and can be customised to suit your needs, your vision, and your celebration style.

Whether you're seeking hands-on planning or refined on-the-day coordination, you'll receive a personalised, considered approach every step of the way.

Full Planning & Day-of Coordination

Designed for couples who want complete support from the very beginning through to the final moments of their wedding day.

This service allows you to enjoy the entire planning process knowing every detail is thoughtfully considered, beautifully organised, and professionally managed. From shaping your vision to delivering a seamless wedding day, I am with you every step of the way.

This service includes:

  • Venue research and recommendations
  • Venue visits to explore your vision, logistics, and possibilities
  • Vendor sourcing, booking, and ongoing management
  • Full planning logistics and detailed coordination
  • A personalised planning checklist
  • Creation of your wedding day timeline and run sheet

  • Complete wedding day coordination and management

On-the-Day Coordination

Designed for couples who have planned their wedding and want a calm, experienced professional to ensure everything runs exactly as intended.

From the weeks leading up to your wedding through to the final moments of the day, I step in to manage the details, coordinate your vendors, and quietly keep everything flowing so you, your family, and your bridal party can be fully present.

This service includes:

  • A dedicated wedding coordinator
  • Venue visit(s) to review your vision, logistics, and responsibilities for the wedding and rehearsal
  • Liaison with all contracted vendors 2–3 weeks prior to your wedding
  • Attendance and coordination of the ceremony rehearsal
  • Oversight of ceremony and reception set-up (pack-down by discussion)
  • Up to 8 hours of wedding day coordination (additional hours available by arrangement)
  • Acting as the main point of contact on the day
  • Ensuring the venue is styled and prepared according to your plans before guest arrival
  • Calm management of any unexpected changes or challenges
